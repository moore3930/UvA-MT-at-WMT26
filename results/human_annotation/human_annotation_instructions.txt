Pairwise Translation Annotation Instructions

You will compare two translations of the same English source segment.

Columns
- `src`: the English source text
- `hypo_A`: translation A
- `hypo_B`: translation B
- `label`: your choice

Your task
1. Read the source and compare `hypo_A` with `hypo_B`.
2. Fill `label` with exactly one of:
   - `A`
   - `B`
   - `tie`
   - `unclear`

Guidelines
- Use `A` if translation A is better overall.
- Use `B` if translation B is better overall.
- Use `tie` if they are effectively equally good.
- Use `unclear` if you cannot judge confidently.

Notes
- The order of A and B is randomized.
- Judge only the text shown in the row.
- Do not use external tools or references.
